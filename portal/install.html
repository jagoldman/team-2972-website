Team 2972 app

Install the app

Put the Team 2972 portal on your phone, tablet or computer. It opens like any other app, and you still sign in the same way.

Back to the portal

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button in the browser bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen , then Add.

If the team turns on notifications later, iPhone needs the app on the Home Screen first.

Android

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install below if you see it, or open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Computer

  1. Open this site in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or press Install below.
  3. Safari on Mac: choose File, Add to Dock.

What you get

  • An icon on your home screen, dock or desktop that opens the portal in one tap.
  • A full-screen window without the browser's address bar.
  • Faster starts, because the app's look and feel is kept on your device.
  • The same sign-in as the website, and the same apps and tiles you see here.
