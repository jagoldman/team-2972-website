The simple rules for using the team site.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What this is

These are the simple rules for using the Team 2972 website and team apps. The team is FIRST Robotics Competition Team 2972, the Gears & Buccaneers, at Dawson School. By using the site you agree to them.

The public website

Anyone may read the public pages. Please do not copy our photos, logos or text to suggest that you are the team, or that the team endorses you. If you want to use something, ask us at robotics@dawsonschool.org.

Team accounts

  • Accounts are for team students, mentors, parents and alumni. An admin approves each one.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. Do not share your account or try to open someone else’s.
  • Do not try to get into parts of the site meant for other roles, and do not try to break or overload it.
  • Be kind and keep it about the team. Do not post anything unlawful, harassing or private about other people.
  • Admins can change or turn off an account, and remove content, to keep the site safe for the team.

What you add

Tasks, comments and notes you add belong to the team’s work. The team uses them to run the robotics program.

AI helpers

If a team admin turns on an AI helper, its answers can be wrong. Check anything important before you rely on it, and do not type passwords or other private information into it.

No guarantees

The site is run by a school robotics team and volunteers. We do our best to keep it working, but it may be down or change without notice, and we provide it as is.

Privacy and contact

What we keep about you is described on the privacy page. Questions: robotics@dawsonschool.org.
