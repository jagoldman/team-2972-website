Privacy

What the team site keeps about you, and why.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who we are

This site belongs to FIRST Robotics Competition Team 2972, the Gears & Buccaneers, at Dawson School. The team runs the public website at 2972robotics.com and a members-only team portal with a few team apps (a task app, a kickoff app and an admin app). Questions about this page: robotics@dawsonschool.org.

Visiting the public website

You do not need an account to read the public pages. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell or share information about visitors. Some pages load photos from Google Drive and videos from YouTube, so Google sees those requests the way it would on any website that shows its content.

Signing in

Team members sign in with Google, using a Dawson account, or with an account the team approved after a request through our request form. When you use Google to sign in, we ask Google only for the basic sign-in details (“openid”, “email” and “profile”). Google shares your name and email address with us. We never see your Google password. A person with an approved account who does not use Google signs in with a password they set themselves.

What the team site keeps

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your role group: student, mentor, parent, alumni or admin. It decides which team apps you can open.
  • What you add in the team apps, such as tasks, comments and kickoff notes.
  • An audit log of account requests, approvals and changes, so the team can see who did what.
  • Session cookies that keep you signed in. They are strictly necessary for signing in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who can see it

Only signed-in team members can open the team apps, and each app shows only what your role allows. Team admins can see account details and the audit log so they can run the team site. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

AI features

The task app and the kickoff app have optional AI helpers (a chat and a research assistant). They are off unless a team admin turns them on. When they are on, what you type in the helper is sent to Google’s Gemini service to get an answer. We do not send your name or user ID with it.

Email

The team site sends email for account approval, password reset and task digests, through the team’s email service.

Where it is kept, and backups

The site runs on one server on Google Cloud in the United States (us-central1). The server makes a database backup every night and keeps the newest 7 daily and 4 weekly copies. It also takes a nightly snapshot of the whole disk and keeps those for 14 days. Because of backups, a deleted account can remain in a backup copy until that copy is replaced.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your account. Ask a team mentor or admin, or write to robotics@dawsonschool.org.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. The terms for using the site are on the terms page.
