Become a Mentor

Volunteers guide our students and make the team possible.

What a mentor does

FIRST describes a mentor as an adult who works with the team in their own area of expertise, for one meeting or for the whole season. Mentors guide the students' process. The students still do the learning, and the work, as far as their skills allow.

Teach and coach

Help students learn new skills, such as CAD, programming, wiring and machining, and grow as people.

Guide, not do

Ask questions and steer how the team solves the season's challenge, without handing over the solution. How hands-on a mentor is changes with the students' experience each year.

Business and outreach

Not every mentor builds robots. Mentors also help with logistics, planning, fundraising and community events.

Support at competition

Advise students between matches. During a match, the student drive team makes the decisions.
